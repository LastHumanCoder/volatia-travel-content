Why Choose Volatia: Travel Concierge Services Backed by Interpreters

Plenty of companies will plan your trip. One of them can also put a professional interpreter on the phone at three in the morning in a language you do not speak.

Choose Volatia when the trip has to work in a language your travellers do not speak. We provide the same travel concierge services as a good agency, planning, booking and looking after the details, and we add professional interpreters in 300+ languages, reachable 24 hours a day, every day of the year, from the same organization.

If your trip is entirely in English and nothing can go wrong, any competent agency will do. If neither of those is true, this is the difference.

The part nobody else offers

Every travel concierge can rebook a flight. The question is what happens in the situations a rebooking does not fix.

  • A traveller in a hospital who cannot describe their symptoms or understand the consent form they are being asked to sign.
  • A negotiation where the other side's English is fine socially and not fine for a liability clause.
  • A police station, a customs desk or an immigration office, where being misunderstood has consequences.
  • A parent at home who needs to reach a program leader through a language barrier and a time zone.

Volatia has been solving exactly these moments since 2003, for hospitals, courts, school districts and government agencies. Travel was the obvious next place to put the same network.

How our concierge travel services are priced

Travel is quoted per trip, against the itinerary we build. Language services run on one of three plans:

  • Annual plan. A committed budget, the lowest rates, suited to organizations that know roughly what they need.
  • Pay as you go. Billed by use, with no commitment, for unpredictable or occasional need.
  • Hybrid. Annual pricing until the budget is used, then pay as you go.

On-demand phone interpreting bills by the minute, so it costs almost nothing until a traveller actually needs it. That is what makes it practical to give every traveller access rather than only the ones with scheduled meetings.

Honest limits

  • In-person interpreting is a United States service. Phone, video and virtual platform interpreting and all translation work worldwide. For an interpreter physically in the room abroad, we arrange it as part of the trip plan rather than claiming blanket global coverage.
  • Travel support runs on business hours, 8am to 5pm Eastern. The language service is the part that runs 24/7/365.
  • We are new to travel. The network behind it is not.

Common questions

What do travel concierge services include?

Planning a custom itinerary, booking every element of the trip, handling changes, and looking after the details a traveller would otherwise chase themselves. Volatia's travel concierge service adds document translation and access to professional interpreters in 300+ languages, 24 hours a day.

How is a travel concierge different from a booking site?

A booking site gives you a search box. A concierge gives you a person who builds the trip around its purpose, books it, and remains reachable when something changes. With Volatia that person sits inside a company that also operates a 24/7 interpreter network.

Do I need to be a large organization to use this?

No. We work with universities, companies and government agencies, and also with families and individuals. The service is the same, scaled to the trip.

What does a personal travel concierge cost?

Travel is quoted per trip against the itinerary. Language services are billed under an annual plan, a pay as you go model, or a hybrid of the two. On-demand phone interpreting bills by the minute, so it costs almost nothing until it is used.

Can you support a language I have never heard of?

Probably. The network covers more than 300 languages by phone and video, including sign languages by video, through more than 18,000 interpreters and translators. Tell us the language and we will confirm before you book.
