The problem a partnership solves

Most universities discover, eventually, that student group travel is happening in more places than anyone is tracking. A faculty led program in one department, a choir tour in another, an athletics trip, a research cohort, a service learning group. Each arranged separately, each with its own vendor, each with a different answer to the question of what happens if something goes wrong.

That is a procurement problem, a risk problem and a duty of care problem at the same time. A single partnership makes all three answerable.

What a partnership covers

  • Every program type. Faculty led, direct enrollment, exchange, athletics, performing arts, service learning, research and conference travel.
  • One contract and one point of contact, rather than a vendor per department.
  • Interpreters in 300+ languages, 24/7/365, available to every travelling group by phone or video, including sign languages by video.
  • Document translation for consent forms, handbooks, emergency procedures and host agreements, kept consistent between cohorts through translation memory.
  • Consistent compliance. FERPA and HIPAA compliance and ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certification apply to every program, not just the ones someone remembered to check.
  • Consolidated reporting, so the institution can see its travel in one place.

Why language belongs in the agreement

Institutions usually buy travel and language separately, if they buy language at all. On a student group trip that gap has a predictable shape: a participant needs medical care, or there is an incident with local authorities, or a host institution meeting cannot proceed, and the faculty lead is trying to source an interpreter from a hotel lobby.

Under a partnership, every travelling group has interpreter access set up before departure, on every participant's phone, in more than 300 languages, at any hour. It bills by the minute, so it costs almost nothing until it is needed.

Common questions

What does a university travel partnership include?

Planning and booking for every program type the institution runs, interpreters in more than 300 languages available 24 hours a day to every travelling group, document translation for consent forms and handbooks, and consistent FERPA, HIPAA and ISO compliance across all of it, under one agreement.

Does a partnership require a competitive procurement process?

Often not. We hold a national cooperative contract covering all 50 states and U.S. territories, which many institutions can purchase through directly. Your procurement office will confirm whether it applies.

Can a partnership cover athletics and performing arts travel?

Yes. Faculty led programs, direct enrollment, exchange, athletics, choir and ensemble tours, service learning, research cohorts and conference travel all sit under the same agreement.

How is student group travel priced?

Travel is quoted per program against the itinerary. Language services run under an annual plan, pay as you go, or a hybrid. On-demand phone interpreting bills by the minute, so equipping every group costs almost nothing until it is used.

Do you work with school districts as well as universities?

Yes. The FERPA obligations are the same and the language requirement is often larger, because participating families may not speak English either. We have served school districts through our language business for many years.
