Business Travel Concierge Services: What Is Actually Included

The word concierge covers everything from a booking desk to a person who will find you a tailor in Milan on a Sunday. Worth knowing which one you are buying.

Business travel concierge services normally cover planning and booking, changes and rebooking, ground transport, restaurant and venue arrangements, and a named contact. What they almost never include is live language support, which is the single thing most likely to matter on an international trip.

Volatia includes it: professional interpreters in 300+ languages, 24 hours a day, from the company that booked the trip.

The three tiers, honestly labelled

TierWhat you getWhat it is really for
Booking deskFlights and hotels booked for you, within policyCost control and compliance
Travel managementThe above plus policy, reporting, duty of care and rebookingRunning a travel programme
ConciergeThe above plus a named person handling the details of each tripSenior travellers and trips that must not fail

Most organizations think they are buying the third and are actually buying the first. The test is simple: ask what happens at two in the morning in a city where nobody speaks your language.

What a business travel concierge should cover

  • Planning built around the meeting, not around the cheapest routing.
  • Booking everything in one place: flights, hotels, cars, transfers, activities.
  • Changes handled for you, including the rebooking nobody wants to do from an airport.
  • Ground logistics: airport transfers, drivers, and knowing which parts of a city are slow at which hours.
  • Venues and hospitality: meeting rooms, restaurants that suit the occasion, dietary and accessibility needs.
  • A named contact who knows the trip, rather than a queue.
  • Documents: visa guidance, invitation letters, itineraries in a usable format.
  • Duty of care: knowing where your travellers are and being able to reach them.

The gap in almost every concierge offering

Run down the list above and you will notice it assumes communication. Every item is about arranging things, and arranging things works fine right up until your traveller has to explain something complicated to somebody who does not share their language.

An international travel concierge can rebook your flight. It cannot, as a rule, help when:

  • Your traveller is in an emergency room describing symptoms.
  • A supplier meeting needs an interpreter at short notice.
  • Your traveller is asked to sign a statement in a language they cannot read.
  • A host institution or counterpart wants to speak to someone and cannot.

Those are the moments a concierge service is nominally for, and they are exactly where the language barrier turns a service into a phone number.

Questions to ask any concierge travel agent

  1. What exactly happens at 2am local time, and who answers?
  2. Do you provide interpreters, or a list of interpreters?
  3. Which languages, and how quickly can you connect one?
  4. Can you translate a contract, and to what standard?
  5. What are your security and compliance certifications?
  6. Is there a single accountable supplier, or a chain of subcontractors?
  7. How is this billed, and what happens if we barely use it?

Common questions

What do business travel concierge services include?

Planning and booking built around the purpose of the trip, changes and rebooking, ground transport, venue and restaurant arrangements, visa guidance, and a named contact who knows the trip. Volatia adds document translation and interpreters in 300+ languages, 24 hours a day.

How is a concierge different from a corporate booking tool?

A booking tool enforces policy and produces tickets. A concierge is a person who builds the trip around what it has to achieve and handles what goes wrong. The difference shows up when something changes at short notice.

Is a travel concierge worth it for business travel?

For routine domestic trips, usually not. For international travel where a meeting matters, where the schedule is complex, or where a language barrier exists, the cost is small against the cost of the trip failing.

What does an international travel concierge cost?

Approaches vary from retainers and memberships to per-trip pricing. Volatia quotes travel per trip and bills language services under an annual plan, pay as you go, or a hybrid, with on-demand interpreting charged by the minute.

Do concierge services include interpreters?

Almost never. Most will refer you to a third-party provider, which means a separate relationship and a separate contract at the moment you least want one. Volatia provides interpreting directly, through its own network of more than 18,000 linguists.
