Common questions

What is Volatia Travel?

A personalized travel planning and booking service run by Volatia Language Network. We plan and book flights, hotels, cars, cruises, activities and vacation packages across 190+ countries, and every traveller can reach a professional interpreter in 300+ languages, 24 hours a day.

How is this different from a normal travel agency?

A travel agency books the trip. We also own the language network behind it. If your traveller lands in a hospital, a police station or a negotiation and cannot make themselves understood, they reach an interpreter through the same company that made the booking, at any hour.

Do I have to use the interpreting service to book travel?

No. You can book travel on its own. Language support is there when the trip needs it, and most travellers ask for it once they understand it is included in the same relationship.

Which languages are covered?

More than 300 by phone and video, including sign languages by video, and more than 280 for in-person interpreting within the United States. Document translation covers the same range.

Can Volatia handle travel for a university or a company?

Yes. Institutional travel is our focus. We hold a national cooperative contract covering all 50 states and U.S. territories, we are ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certified, and we are HIPAA and FERPA compliant, which is usually what a procurement office asks about first.
