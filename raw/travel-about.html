About Volatia Travel: Why an Interpretation Services Company Books Trips

Our founder spent seven years as a refugee in five countries. The company he built exists because he knows exactly what it costs not to be understood.

Volatia Language Network is an interpretation services company founded in 2003, providing interpreting and translation in 300+ languages through a network of more than 18,000 linguists. Volatia Travel applies that network to travel: we plan and book the trip, and the same interpreters are available to your travellers 24 hours a day while they are on it.

We did not add travel because it looked like a good market. We added it because the failures we were already fixing kept happening to people who were away from home.

Our founder

Baraka Kasongo founded Volatia and leads it as CEO. Before any of it, he was a refugee in five different countries over a period of seven years.

That is not a line in a brochure. It is the reason the company exists and the reason it is organised the way it is. Being unable to explain yourself to a doctor, an official or a teacher is not an inconvenience. It determines what happens to you. Baraka built Volatia to remove that barrier from as many encounters as possible.

He holds an MBA from Liberty University, a BSc in Management and Leadership from Bluefield College, and an associate degree in Emergency Management from Frederick Community College, and he is a certified Green Belt Lean Six Sigma practitioner through Virginia Tech. He serves on several boards focused on diversity, equity and inclusion.

The language services behind the travel

As an interpretation services company we provide six things, and Volatia Travel draws on all of them.

  • Phone interpreting connected in seconds with an access code, the backbone of our remote interpretation services.
  • Video interpreting for anything where seeing the person matters, including sign language.
  • Virtual platform interpreting on Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet, scheduled in advance.
  • Onsite interpreting in 280+ languages across the United States.
  • Translation of documents, subtitles, captions, transcription and voice-over.
  • Translation memory, so repeated content is never paid for twice.

Among phone interpreting companies and video interpretation services, what distinguishes us is not the list. It is that the same organization now also books the trip.

Why travel, and why now

Because the pattern was obvious once we looked. The interpreter calls that came in at unusual hours, from unfamiliar places, about hospitals and police stations and airports, were from people who were travelling. They had arranged the trip with one company and the language with another, or more often with nobody at all.

Joining the two removes a gap that only exists because of how the industry is organised. A traveller should not have to source an interpreter in a foreign city at midnight, and they certainly should not have to do it while the thing has already gone wrong.

What we will not claim

Volatia Travel is new. We are not going to imply that our twenty years, our 18,000 linguists or our 99% satisfaction rating are travel credentials, because they are not. They are language credentials, and they are real and verifiable.

What we will claim is this: no other travel provider owns an interpreter network like ours, and for a trip that crosses a language barrier that is the thing that matters most.
