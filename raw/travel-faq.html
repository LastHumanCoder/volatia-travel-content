Volatia Travel FAQ: Our Travel Booking Solution Explained

What you can book, how the language support works, what it costs and where the limits are. Answers rather than brochure copy.

Volatia Travel is a travel booking solution with an interpreter network attached. You can book flights, hotels, rental cars, cruises, activities and vacation packages across 190+ countries, and every traveller can reach a professional interpreter in 300+ languages, 24 hours a day, from the same company.

Booking

What can I book through Volatia Travel?

Flights, hotels, rental cars, cruises, activities and full vacation packages, across more than 190 countries. You can browse and book yourself, or have us plan a custom itinerary and book it for you.

Can I book online or do I have to speak to someone?

Both. Our online travel booking solution lets you search and book directly. For anything with moving parts, a group program, a multi-city business trip, a trip with language requirements, talking to a planner will get you a better result.

Do you handle group and institutional bookings?

Yes, and they are our focus. Business and negotiation travel, faculty led study abroad, university and school group programs, and corporate travel management all sit under our institutional service.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as the trip is certain, particularly for group travel and for anything needing scheduled onsite or simultaneous interpreting, which should be arranged weeks in advance.

Language support

How does interpreter access actually work?

Before departure we set up access on each traveller's phone. When they need an interpreter they connect by phone or video, usually within seconds, and are matched to a professional in the language they need. It runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

Which languages are covered?

More than 300 by phone and video, including sign languages by video. More than 280 for in-person interpreting. Document translation covers the same range. If you have an unusual language pair, ask and we will confirm before you book.

Can an interpreter join a scheduled meeting?

Yes. Virtual platform interpreting covers Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and similar tools, booked in advance with the meeting link. For in-person meetings we arrange consecutive interpreting, and for conferences, simultaneous.

Is in-person interpreting available everywhere?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. Phone, video and virtual platform interpreting and all translation work worldwide. In-person interpreting is provided directly across the United States in 280+ languages. For an interpreter physically present abroad, we arrange it as part of the trip plan.

Do I have to buy language services to book travel?

No. Travel can be booked on its own. Most clients add language support once they understand it bills by the minute and costs almost nothing until someone uses it.

Cost

How is travel priced?

Per trip, against the itinerary we build, with real prices for each element rather than an opaque package figure.

How are language services priced?

Three plans. An annual plan with a committed budget, which carries the lowest rates. Pay as you go, billed by use, with no commitment. Or a hybrid that starts on annual pricing and moves to pay as you go once the budget is spent.

Is on-demand interpreting expensive to make available?

No, because it bills by the minute. Giving every traveller on a program access costs very little until one of them actually needs it, which is what makes it practical to equip everyone rather than only the people with scheduled meetings.

Changes, problems and support

What are your support hours?

Travel support is reachable during business hours, 8am to 5pm Eastern. The language service runs 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, which is the part that matters at 3am in a hospital.

What happens if something goes wrong mid-trip?

For a language problem, the traveller connects to an interpreter immediately, at any hour. For a travel problem, contact us during business hours and we work the change. We are straightforward that these two run on different clocks.

Can you change or cancel a booking?

Subject to the terms of the airline, hotel or operator involved, which always apply on top of ours. We will tell you what the conditions are before you commit rather than after.

Institutions and procurement

Are you compliant enough for a university or a health system?

We are ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certified and HIPAA and FERPA compliant, which comes from twenty years of work with health systems, courts and school districts rather than from the travel business.

How does public sector procurement work?

We hold a national cooperative contract covering all 50 states and U.S. territories, which for many public institutions removes the need for a separate competitive process.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes. If a language session does not meet the standard, we provide another of equivalent value at no cost.
